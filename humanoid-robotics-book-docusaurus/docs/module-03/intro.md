# Module 3: Humanoid Robot Engineering

## Overview

This module focuses on the core engineering challenges of humanoid robotics: locomotion, manipulation, and motion planning. You'll learn the algorithms and techniques that enable robots to walk, grasp objects, and navigate complex environments.

## Module Learning Outcomes

By completing this module, you will:

- ✅ Implement bipedal locomotion algorithms (ZMP, CPG, MPC)
- ✅ Design grasping and manipulation strategies
- ✅ Apply motion planning algorithms (RRT, PRM, optimization-based)
- ✅ Integrate whole-body control for coordinated movement

## Chapters

### [Chapter 7: Humanoid Locomotion Systems](/docs/module-03/chapter-07)
Master bipedal walking, running, and dynamic balance control.

### [Chapter 8: Manipulation & Grasping](/docs/module-03/chapter-08)
Implement force control, grasp planning, and dexterous manipulation.

### [Chapter 9: Motion Planning & Navigation](/docs/module-03/chapter-09)
Apply sampling-based and optimization-based planning for collision-free motion.

---

## Prerequisites

- **Dynamics**: Rigid body mechanics, Lagrangian formulation
- **Optimization**: Gradient descent, constrained optimization
- **Linear Algebra**: Matrix operations, eigenvalues

## Estimated Time

**Duration**: 4-5 weeks (15-20 hours/week)
