# Introduction to Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

Welcome to **Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics: Bridging Digital Intelligence and Embodied Systems**—a comprehensive, professional-level textbook designed to guide you from theoretical foundations to practical implementation of humanoid robotic systems.

## 🎯 Course Objectives

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

- **Design and implement** complete robotic systems using ROS 2 (Humble)
- **Apply advanced mathematics** (kinematics, dynamics, control theory) to real-world robotics problems
- **Integrate perception systems** including computer vision, SLAM, and sensor fusion
- **Develop locomotion and manipulation** algorithms for humanoid platforms
- **Deploy sim-to-real** transfer techniques for physical robot deployment

## 📚 Course Structure

This textbook is organized into **4 comprehensive modules**, each containing **3 chapters** with **3 lessons** per chapter:

### Module 1: Foundations of Embodied Intelligence
Learn the core infrastructure—ROS 2 architecture, robot modeling (URDF), simulation environments, and sensor interfaces.

### Module 2: Perception & Understanding
Master computer vision, SLAM algorithms, probabilistic robotics, and human-robot interaction.

### Module 3: Motion & Control
Dive deep into actuation, inverse kinematics, bipedal locomotion, and manipulation planning.

### Module 4: Integrated Systems & Deployment
Bring it all together with system integration, sim-to-real transfer, and advanced topics like reinforcement learning.

## 🎓 Target Audience

This course is designed for:
- **Engineering students** (undergraduate/graduate) in robotics, computer science, or mechanical engineering
- **Researchers** exploring embodied AI and physical intelligence
- **Hobbyists and practitioners** with Python programming experience and basic robotics knowledge

## 🛠️ Prerequisites

- **Programming**: Proficiency in Python; familiarity with C++ is beneficial
- **Mathematics**: Linear algebra, calculus, basic probability
- **Robotics**: Understanding of coordinate frames and basic kinematics (helpful but not required)

## 💡 Learning Approach

Each lesson follows a rigorous academic structure:

1. **Learning Objectives**: Clear, measurable goals
2. **Theoretical Foundation**: Mathematical rigor with detailed explanations
3. **Practical Implementation**: Production-grade ROS 2 code examples
4. **Technical Deep Dive**: Advanced concepts (e.g., quaternion math, MPC optimization)
5. **Assignments**: Challenging exercises to solidify understanding

## 🚀 Capstone Project: The "Antigravity" Humanoid

The course culminates in a comprehensive capstone project where you'll design, simulate, and program a simplified humanoid robot to perform a **Pick-and-Place task** in a cluttered environment, integrating all skills learned throughout the modules.
