# Module 1: Foundations of Physical AI

## Overview

Physical AI represents the convergence of artificial intelligence with embodied systems—robots that exist in and interact with the physical world. This module establishes the theoretical and practical foundations necessary to understand how intelligence manifests in physical form.

## Module Learning Outcomes

By completing this module, you will:

- ✅ Understand the fundamental principles of Physical AI and embodied cognition
- ✅ Master the essential components of humanoid robotic systems
- ✅ Apply cognitive models to robotic behavior and decision-making
- ✅ Design systems that bridge digital intelligence with physical action

## Chapters

### [Chapter 1: Introduction to Physical AI](/docs/module-01/chapter-01)
Explore the paradigm shift from disembodied AI to physical intelligence, examining historical context, current state-of-the-art, and future trajectories.

### [Chapter 2: Humanoid Robotics Essentials](/docs/module-01/chapter-02)
Master the mechanical, electrical, and software components that comprise humanoid robots, from actuators to control architectures.

### [Chapter 3: Embodiment & Cognitive Models](/docs/module-01/chapter-03)
Investigate how physical embodiment shapes cognition, learning, and behavior in robotic systems.

## Prerequisites

- **Mathematics**: Linear algebra, calculus, basic probability theory
- **Programming**: Python proficiency; C++ familiarity helpful
- **Physics**: Classical mechanics, basic dynamics

## Estimated Time

**Duration**: 3-4 weeks (12-15 hours/week)
